Questions

Is this financial advice?+

No. It is arithmetic on the numbers you enter, and it says so on the page. It cannot see your income, your other debts or your situation — it shows you what your own numbers imply.

What if I have several debts?+

Enter the combined balance and a weighted average APR and the date will be close. The paid plan covers which one to attack first and why.

Do I get updates?+

Yes, and it is a web page — improvements appear automatically with no download.
