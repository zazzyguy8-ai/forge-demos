Questions

Isn't it one inch of fish per gallon?+

No. That rule treats a 10 cm goldfish and ten 1 cm tetras as the same load, and they are nowhere close. This works from waste output, adult size, filtration and plants instead.

My fish isn't on the list.+

Then the tool knows nothing about it and will not pretend to. It covers common, well-documented species. If you follow a page that built this for a specialist niche, ask them to add your species — it takes minutes.

Why does the free version already tell me the problems?+

Because a tool that hides whether your fish will kill each other would be a bad tool. You pay for the stocking plan, the capacity numbers and the order to add them in.
