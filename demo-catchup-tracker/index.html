Questions

Does it remember where I am?+

Yes — your ticked arcs are saved in this browser, so you can come back and update as you go. That is the point of it rather than a one-off calculation.

Is the filler list exact?+

No, and it says so. Filler is counted per arc as an estimate, which is enough for planning. For skipping specific episodes, use a dedicated filler guide alongside this.

What about the ongoing arc?+

It is counted to the episode number this was built against, and it grows weekly. If you are far in the future, add the difference.
