Questions

Is this just a TDEE calculator?+

The free number is, and that part is genuinely free — use it and leave if that is all you need. What you pay for is everything after the number: the macros, the week-by-week adjustments, and the protocol for when you stall, which is where nearly everyone actually fails.

What if the number is wrong for me?+

It will be slightly wrong for everyone — BMR formulas carry about a 10% error. That is exactly why the plan is built around correcting it from your own 7-day weight average instead of trusting the formula.

Do I need a subscription?+

No. One payment, yours forever, and it works offline once loaded.

Does it work for women?+

Yes — the equation branches on sex and the protein target scales with your bodyweight.
