Questions

Isn't more clothes just more outfits?+

No, and that is the point. Six tops and two bottoms make fewer outfits than four and four, even though both are eight items. The tool shows you which side you are over-buying.

How accurate is the number?+

It is an estimate — it assumes your pieces broadly coordinate and cannot see your actual clothes. The ranking of what to buy next is the reliable part, because it compares categories against each other rather than claiming an absolute.

Do I need to enter every item?+

Just counts per category, which takes a minute. Nothing is stored anywhere but your own browser.
