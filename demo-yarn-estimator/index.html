Questions

Why is crochet more than knitting?+

Because it is — crochet stitches use noticeably more yarn to cover the same area, around 30% more for comparable fabric. Most estimators ignore this and that is why crocheters constantly run short.

How accurate is it?+

Within roughly 20%, which is why the total includes a buffer and why it tells you to swatch. If you already have a pattern, trust the pattern — this is for designing your own or substituting yarn.

Does it cover my specific yarn?+

Enter your ball's actual yardage and price and it works from those. Yardage per ball varies hugely between brands even at the same weight.
